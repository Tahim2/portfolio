Publications

Papers & Research Work

Accepted Conference Paper

RXV: Interpretable RF-Stacked CNNs for Brain Tumor MRI Classification

This paper introduces RXV, a brain tumor MRI classification framework that combines ResNet50V2, VGG16, and Xception with a Random Forest meta-learner. The work emphasizes both strong predictive accuracy and interpretability through Grad-CAM, making it relevant for practical computer-aided diagnostic support.

IEEE WIECON-ECE 2025 — Accepted

Journal Manuscript

A High-Accuracy Stacking Ensemble Model for Precise Thyroid Disease Detection with SHAP and LIME Explanations

This manuscript presents an interpretable ensemble learning approach for thyroid disease detection using Random Forest, XGBoost, and Decision Tree models with a regularized Logistic Regression meta-learner. It focuses on combining high diagnostic performance with clinically meaningful explanations through SHAP and LIME.

Informatics in Medicine Unlocked (Elsevier) — Under Review

Preprint

An Explainable Stacking Ensemble Framework for Early Heart Attack Risk Detection

This work develops an explainable machine learning pipeline for early heart attack risk prediction using feature selection, class balancing, and stacking ensembles. It highlights how predictive modeling and interpretability can work together to support earlier intervention in healthcare settings.
