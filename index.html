Papers & Research Work

Published Conference Paper

RXV: Interpretable RF-Stacked CNNs for Brain Tumor MRI Classification

This paper introduces RXV, an interpretable brain tumor MRI classification framework that integrates ResNet50V2, VGG16, and Xception with a Random Forest meta-learner. The work emphasizes both predictive performance and visual interpretability through Grad-CAM, making it relevant for computer-aided diagnostic research and clinically oriented medical imaging applications.

IEEE WIECON-ECE 2025 — Published in IEEE Xplore

IEEE Xplore

Published Journal Article

A High-Accuracy Stacking Ensemble Model for Precise Thyroid Disease Detection with SHAP and LIME Explanations

This article presents an interpretable ensemble learning framework for thyroid disease detection using Random Forest, XGBoost, and Decision Tree classifiers with a regularized Logistic Regression meta-learner. The study focuses on combining high diagnostic performance with clinically meaningful model explanations through SHAP and LIME, supporting transparent and evidence-oriented AI-assisted disease prediction.

Informatics in Medicine Unlocked — Published by Elsevier / ScienceDirect

ScienceDirect

Preprint / ResearchGate

An Explainable Stacking Ensemble Framework for Early Heart Attack Risk Detection

This work develops an explainable machine learning pipeline for early heart attack risk prediction using feature selection, class balancing, and stacking ensemble techniques. It highlights how predictive modeling and interpretability can work together to support earlier risk identification, preventive decision-making, and healthcare-oriented AI research.
